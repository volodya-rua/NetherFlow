NetherFlow is a free, open-source music player with a look of its own. Search any song, play it in full, and follow its lyrics word by word over moving, music-reactive backgrounds.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Search songs, artists and albums, and play them in full, with typos fixed and suggestions as you type</li>
<li>Synced lyrics, word by word where available, with 19 lyric animations (Wave, Spiral, Shatter, Fireworks, Swarm, Black Hole, Cylinder, Globe, Helix, DNA, Tornado, Knot, Möbius, Ribbon, Orbit, Atom, Tunnel, Cube and Classic)</li>
<li>5 themes that change the whole look at once, with a backdrop, timeline and cursor of their own: Stickman (notebook paper, stick figures dancing to the beat), Spider (a comic book at night), Blocks (a blocky world at dusk), Tactical (an agent by the lyrics, a round timer) and Ops (a desert bomb site, smoke and tracers, a HUD)</li>
<li>27 animated backgrounds, including fractals, galaxies and a plasma globe, that take their colors from the album cover, or a still gradient that is lightest on slower phones</li>
<li>A beat ring and screen edges that move with the music</li>
<li>Equalizer with presets, a volume booster, and each song at its original loudness</li>
<li>Autoplay: when the list ends, songs like the one playing carry on, in its language and era, learning from what you skip and like</li>
<li>Smooth mix: songs blend into each other on the beat, like a DJ mix</li>
<li>Sing: karaoke, with the voice turned down as far as you like and the lyrics big</li>
<li>Import playlists from Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube by pasting the link</li>
<li>Your stats: minutes, plays, a streak, top songs, artists and genres, and a card to share</li>
<li>Listen together: up to 10 people hear the same song at the same moment, end-to-end encrypted</li>
<li>Phone ↔ PC sync of your library over your own Wi-Fi, with a 6-number code and no account</li>
<li>A home-screen widget, lyrics on the lock screen, and liked songs saved for offline on Wi-Fi by themselves</li>
<li>Liked songs, playlists, queue and history</li>
<li>Save songs for offline listening, one at a time or a whole playlist at once</li>
<li>Make a short video clip of a song with its lyrics for your story</li>
<li>No account, no ads of its own, no analytics or tracking</li>
</ul>

<b>How it works</b>
NetherFlow doesn't host any music or lyrics. It finds songs in Apple's iTunes catalog and YouTube Music, plays them through YouTube's embedded player (or from Audius at 320 kbps when the artist's own upload is there), and gets lyrics from LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music, Kugou and YouTube Music (lyrics by LyricFind). Saving for offline downloads the audio from YouTube with yt-dlp.
