In short: NetherFlow is a free, open-source music player made by Volodya.rua (MIT License). It doesn't host any music, videos or lyrics: it finds them on other services (YouTube and YouTube Music, Audius, Apple's iTunes catalog, LRCLIB, NetEase Cloud Music and Kugou) and shows them to you directly from those services. If you choose to save a song for offline listening, the app downloads it from YouTube and keeps it inside the app, on your device only. NetherFlow has no accounts, no ads of its own and no analytics, and the developer receives none of your data.

Saved songs stay inside the app on your device, for your own personal listening only: you must not extract, copy, share, sell or distribute them.

Privacy Policy

Summary

NetherFlow has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads of its own, and since its source code is public, anyone can check that. The developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data. To work, the app talks directly from your device to the third-party services listed below, which receive the information described there under their own privacy policies.

Stored only on your device

  • Your settings: mood, background and its quality (or the quality the app picked for your device), lyric animation (and Extreme lyrics, their look, emoji and music video), font, size and alignment, timeline and beat styles, the island and its glow, volume, repeat, equalizer and loudness.
  • Lyric-sync adjustments you made for individual songs.
  • Your search history: recent searches and recently played songs (you can clear it in the search screen).
  • Your library: liked songs and playlists (and playlists you import).
  • Your listening stats: time listened by day, hour, song, artist and genre (Library › Your stats).
  • Autoplay's memory: the songs you've heard lately (so they aren't picked again soon) and how much you like each artist it picks, from what you skip, play through or like.
  • Songs you saved for offline listening, with their covers and, if you chose, their lyrics (you can remove them in the Library).
  • That you accepted these terms, and which version.
  • Phone ↔ PC sync copies your library, history and stats directly between your own devices on your local network, encrypted with a key made from the code shown: it never goes through anyone else's server. Listen together sends only what the session needs (the song, where it's at, names, reactions), encrypted, through a public relay.

This stays on your device and is never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes it.
