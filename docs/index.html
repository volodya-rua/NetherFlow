NetherFlow: turn silence into song

NetherFlow

turn silence into song.

yo, this is NetherFlow. A free music player for Windows, Linux, Android and your TV that turns any song into a whole vibe: full songs, lyrics hitting word by word, and backgrounds that actually move with the music.

Search any song and it plays the whole thing.

Songs, artists, albums, typos and all. When your list runs out it keeps going with songs like the one playing, in the same language and era, and it learns from what you skip.

These are running live in your browser right now, the same shader code the app uses. There are 27 of them, and in the app they take their colours from the album cover.

Fractal

A kaleidoscopic fractal, six-fold mirrored, folding in on itself forever.

This is it on a PC.

Home over Black Hole, Settings over Aurora, the equalizer over Liquid, and the theme picker. YouTube turns loud songs down, so NetherFlow plays everything at its original loudness, and there's an equalizer and a booster if you want more.

And on your phone.

Android 7.0 and up, and Android TV and Google TV too, worked with the remote. There's a home-screen widget, the lyrics show up on your lock screen and your car's display, and your liked songs save themselves for offline when you're on Wi-Fi.

Every word lands right on the beat. You don't read it, you feel it. Turn silence into song.

The lyrics are synced to the exact recording that's playing, word by word. There are 19 ways for them to come in, and Spiral, Helix and Shatter are my favourites. Karaoke mode turns the voice down, and on Windows, Extreme lyrics cut like a music video.

Windows, Linux and Android

New in 1.4: podcasts, audiobooks, and NetherFlow on your TV.

Every show in Apple's podcast directory and thousands of free audiobooks, right next to your music. It remembers where you stopped, plays them at any speed, and saves episodes for offline. And it's on Android TV and Google TV now: the whole app works with the remote.

Also in there: Explore, with your country's charts, every mood and genre, and mixes made from what you play; playlists you edit together with friends, live; headphone correction for 6,000+ headphones; and NetherFlow on Linux. Plus everything from before: Listen together, playlists as QR codes, phone and PC sync, and your song on your Discord profile.

I came up with what NetherFlow should be, picked every theme and every background, tested every build on my PC and my phone, and kept saying "nah, make it crazier" until it was right. It's free, with no account, no ads and no tracking.
