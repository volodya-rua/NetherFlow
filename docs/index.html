Listening

  • Search anything and play it in full, typos fixed as you type
  • Autoplay that learns from what you skip and like
  • Smooth mix: songs blend like a DJ set, on the beat
  • Equalizer, a booster, and every song at its original loudness
  • Save for offline, one song or a whole playlist
  • Import playlists of any length from Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube

Lyrics

  • Synced word by word to the exact recording
  • Extreme lyrics: 58 looks, emoji, the music video behind
  • Sing: karaoke, the voice turned down
  • Story clips sized for your Instagram story

With your people

  • Listen together: up to 10 people, same song, same moment, end-to-end encrypted
  • Phone ↔ PC sync over your own Wi-Fi with a 6-number code
  • Discord status with cover and progress
  • Your stats: minutes, streaks, top songs, a card to share

Windows · Android

  • A now-playing island over every app on Windows
  • A home-screen widget and lyrics on the lock screen on Android
  • Updates in the app, signed and checked first
  • Minimized, it draws nothing at all: easy on your battery

Human × Claude

NetherFlow is a HuCla build. The idea, the vision, how it should look and feel, that's me, Volodya.rua: every theme, every background, every build tested on my PC and my phone until it was right.

The code is mostly Claude, Anthropic's AI: the player, the lyric engine, the shaders (this page's background is one of them, running live), the 3D races, the Android app. Every commit Claude worked on says so.
